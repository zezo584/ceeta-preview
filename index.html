Our Core Services

Lifting Inspection

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Assessing the integrity of materials, welds and equipment with VT, MT, UT, RT and PT — without damaging the tested items.

Tubular Inspection

Inspection of drilling tubulars and BHA components to API RP7, T.H. Hill DS-1, NS-2 or client-specified standards.

Learn more
