# CEETA for Petroleum and Engineering Services — new website (v1.0.1)

Bilingual corporate website for **CEETA** — petroleum inspection, testing, engineering and HSE training.

**Live preview:** https://zezo584.github.io/ceeta-preview/
**Arabic preview:** https://zezo584.github.io/ceeta-preview/ar/

## Languages

- English (primary) — `/`
- Arabic (RTL) — `/ar/`

## What's in this repo

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html`, `*.html`, `services/`, `ar/` + `assets/` | Clickable preview of the whole site (13 pages × 2 languages). |
| `ceeta-theme.zip` | Installable WordPress theme, v1.0.1 (Appearance → Themes → Upload Theme). |
| `wordpress-theme/ceeta/` | Theme source code. |

## Pages

- Home — animated hero slider (one slide per service), ISO badges, services, certifications, why CEETA, academy, inquiry form
- About Us — who we are, vision, values
- Technical Services + 7 service pages: Lifting Inspection · NDT · Tubular Inspection · CAT III & CAT IV Rig Inspection · Rig Auditing & Commissioning · Lifting Planning & Supervision · Dropped Objects Surveying
- CEETA Academy — 35 courses (Lifting 7 · Operators 8 · Safety 20 in 4 groups) with filters, search and WhatsApp inquiry; 3D training photo slider
- Certifications — ISO 9001:2015 · ISO 45001:2018 · ISO 14001:2015 + EFCBC, ASTM, Chamber of Petroleum and Mining
- Contact Us — phone, WhatsApp, email, form, map

## WordPress theme highlights

- Activating the theme creates every page (EN + AR), the front page and pretty permalinks
- Arabic served under `/ar/` by the theme itself — no translation plugin
- Built-in SEO: titles & descriptions per page and language, canonical, hreflang, Open Graph, JSON-LD (Organization, Service, Course, Breadcrumbs), bilingual sitemap at `/ceeta-sitemap.xml`
- Inquiry form → email + saved under Dashboard → Inquiries, plus WhatsApp button
- Customizer (CEETA Options): contact details, social links, hero images, form recipient, SEO, preloader
- Training gallery picker on the CEETA Academy page
