Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

Assessing the integrity of materials, welds and equipment with VT, MT, UT, RT and PT — without damaging the tested items.

Our Non-Destructive Testing services help assess the integrity and condition of materials, components and equipment without causing damage to the tested items.

Five core NDT methods under one provider
Weld examination for cracks and discontinuities
Ultrasonic thickness measurement for tanks, pipes and equipment

Our testing methods include

VTVisual Testing
MTMagnetic Particle Testing
UTUltrasonic Testing
RTRadiographic Testing
PTLiquid Penetrant Testing

Additional inspection activities include weld examination and ultrasonic thickness measurements for tanks, pipes and other equipment.
